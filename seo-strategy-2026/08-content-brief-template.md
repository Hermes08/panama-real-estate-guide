## Blocking gates

The brief does not go to a writer, and the draft does not go to publish, unless all three pass.

**Gate 1 — Differentiation.** Name at least one thing this page will contain that no top-10 result contains. "More comprehensive" and "better written" do not count. If you cannot name it, the page is not worth writing yet.

**Gate 2 — Sourcing.** Every figure in section 6 has a primary source and a retrieval date. Any figure that can't be sourced is cut, not softened.

**Gate 3 — First-hand.** The page contains at least one element that could only come from being in Panama: a photograph taken on site, a named person interviewed, a real transaction, a document actually filed. Without this, the page is competing on the same footing as every broker blog — which is the position the site is currently in.
