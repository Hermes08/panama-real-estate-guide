# Tone of voice

## Posture
Editorial / market-analyst, not broker. We are the journalist with relationships, not the salesperson with a quota. Think Bloomberg real-estate desk, not Zillow blog.

## Voice rules
- Direct and specific. Lead with the surprising fact, not the setup.
- Numbers over adjectives. "Coronado sold out through Phase III" beats "Coronado is hot."
- Cite the source for any non-obvious claim (gov data, developer, transaction record). If we can't cite it, we either soften the claim or drop it.
- Second person ("you") sparingly, mostly for buyer-decision moments. Default to impersonal / analyst voice.
- Sentences vary: short punch openers, medium analysis sentences, occasional long sentences for nuance. No staccato spam, no run-ons.
- One opinion per article, defended with evidence. Not "objective" mush.

## Length
- Market reports: 1500-2500 words
- Zone guides: 1500-2200 words
- Legal/visa explainers: 1800-3000 words
- Per-project pages: 800-1400 words
- News briefs: 400-700 words

## Phrases that should appear often
- "Reserve from $5,000" (the CTA, when relevant)
- "developer-direct"
- "two oceans, one country"
- Specific zone names (not "the beach"), specific developer names (not "the developer")
- USD prefix on every dollar figure ("USD $480k", not "$480k")

## Banned habits
- Em dashes anywhere (use colons, commas, parentheses, or split sentences)
- Exclamation marks except in actual quotes
- "Discover" / "Unlock" / "Imagine" / "Picture this" openers
- Listicle-only structure ("5 reasons to buy in Boquete") without an analytical spine
- Generic stock-photo metaphors ("hidden gem," "best-kept secret")
- All-caps headings
- Emojis (the brand voice doesn't use them)
- Hard-sell urgency ("act now," "limited time")
- Vague qualifiers without numbers ("very affordable," "great ROI")

## Language conventions
- US English for English content
- Castilian Spanish for ES content
- Brazilian Portuguese for PT content
- German (de_DE) for DE content
- Currency: always prefix "USD" on first reference in any article (multilingual audience needs disambiguation since most LATAM countries also use "$")
- Dates: ISO (YYYY-MM-DD) in tables/data; "Month DD, YYYY" in prose for English; "DD de Month de YYYY" in Spanish
- Place names: keep accents in Spanish (Pedasí, Coclé, Boquete, Casco Viejo); use the local spelling, not anglicized
