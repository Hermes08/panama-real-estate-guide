# Competitors

## Direct competitors (same offer: developer-direct or buyer's-agency for Panama new construction)
Most Panama brokerages are dual-agency MLS-style operations, which means the structural buyer's-agency angle is itself a moat. Direct competitors in the developer-direct + editorial space are thin:

- **Punta Pacifica Realty** (Panama City focus, high-rise condos, broker-led not editorial-led)
- **Casa Solution** (Boquete/Chiriquí highlands focus, expat-oriented, broker-led)
- **Panama Equity** (mid-market Panama City, content-savvy but resale-heavy)
- **Inside Panama Real Estate** (general broker, some content)

What we do better: editorial depth, multilingual coverage (EN/ES/PT/DE), explicit buyer's-agency positioning, developer-direct on 13 projects.

## Indirect competitors (same audience: foreigners researching Panama)
- **International Living**: broad lifestyle content for retirees abroad, Panama is one of many destinations. Wide, shallow. We are narrow, deep.
- **Live and Invest Overseas**: same shape as International Living; seminars and reports for would-be expats. Lead-gen heavy, not transaction-focused.
- **Expat Exchange / Expat.com**: forum communities, user-generated. No transaction layer.
- **YouTube expats-in-Panama channels** (multiple): strong on lifestyle, weak on transaction execution.

## Listing portals (different shape but same audience moments)
- **Encuentra24**: Panama's main listing portal. Resale-heavy, no curation.
- **Vivanuncios**: LATAM-wide. Same shape.
- **Compreapartamento / property aggregators**: auto-listed inventory.

## Topics they've covered well (we should NOT duplicate)
- "Should I retire in Panama?" International Living owns this, no point fighting them on it head-on. We win on the next layer: "Now that you've decided Panama, here's where and how."
- "10 cheapest places to live in Panama": listicle territory, low-margin, content-farm style.
- Generic visa overviews: every site has one. Ours wins by being more specific (per-program, per-buyer-origin).

## Topics nobody covers well (our opportunity)
- **Per-project deep dives** with actual unit availability, real photos, real floor plans (most competitors show stock renderings only)
- **Investment math by zone for a specific buyer profile** ("USD $500k for a Colombian buyer in Costa del Este: rental yield, tax treatment, residency path")
- **Cross-segment articles** (condo vs finca vs commercial for the same capital): broker silos prevent this
- **Multilingual depth**: most competitors are English-only or Spanish-only. We cover EN/ES/PT/DE with proper localization, not auto-translation
- **Comparative project pages** (Cavarossa vs Allure vs Empire, like-for-like): brokers won't write these because they're loyal to one developer
- **Decision-stage content for LATAM HNW** (not "should I move?" but "I'm comparing Panama vs Miami vs Punta del Este")
- **Operating reality**: what daily life is actually like in Costa del Este vs Casco Viejo vs Boquete (commute times, supermarket, healthcare, schools, internet, electricity reliability)
- **Post-purchase reality**: property management, HOA disputes, tenant law, exit options
