# Business: panamarealestateguide.com

## What it is
A multilingual (EN/ES/PT/DE) Panama real estate guide and developer-direct reservation marketplace. Publishes long-form market reports, zone guides, legal/visa explainers, and per-project pages for luxury and mid-luxury developments across Caribbean, Pacific, Azuero and highland Panama. Buyers reserve units with a USD $5,000 refundable deposit, then convert to a full purchase. Differentiator: editorial depth (data-driven market reports, not listing-portal noise) + first-party project relationships (the 13 featured developments are developer-direct, not MLS aggregation).

## In scope
- Per-project pages for the 13 featured developments (Cavarossa, Allure, Bioma, Buenaventura, Empire, Margaritaville Playa Caracol, Pino Alto, Towncenter Boquete, Playa Escondida, Mova by B&B Italia, SilverBay, The Westin Residences Playa Bonita, Altos del María)
- Zone guides (Panama City neighborhoods, Boquete, Bocas, Coronado, Pedasí, Coclé, Punta Pacifica, Costa del Este, Casco Viejo)
- Legal / visa explainers (Friendly Nations, Pensionado, Qualified Investor, residency 2026)
- Tax + financing guides for foreign buyers (USD, property tax, mortgage)
- Market reports (quarterly + annual outlook, supply/demand by coast)
- Moving-to-Panama guides by buyer origin (US retirees, CO/MX/ES/BR investors)
- Lifestyle / daily-living content (cost of living, healthcare, schools)

## Out of scope
- Resale listings from other brokers (we do developer-direct only)
- Vacation rental booking
- Country-specific content for markets we don't serve (EU outside ES, ME, Asia)
- Construction / contractor / interior design how-to
- Property management how-to
- Crypto / NFT / fractional ownership angles
