# Brand guidelines

## Brand spellings (exact)
- panamarealestateguide.com (all lowercase, one word, .com)
- PanamaRealEstateGuide.com (camel case is acceptable in display contexts: page titles, signatures)
- "Panama Real Estate Guide" (display name, three words capitalized)
- David Aguirre (founder, no middle initial)
- Jesbelyn Gutierrez (no accent)
- Panama (no accent in English text; "Panamá" with accent in Spanish/Portuguese)
- Use US spelling: "neighborhood" not "neighbourhood", "color" not "colour"

## Banned words and phrases
- Em dashes ( — ) anywhere. Use colons, commas, parentheses, or split sentences.
- "Hidden gem", "best-kept secret", "diamond in the rough" — every Panama article uses these. We do not.
- "Discover", "Unlock", "Imagine", "Picture this" as openers.
- "Act now", "limited time", "selling fast", "don't miss out" — hard-sell urgency.
- "Guaranteed" applied to investment returns, residency outcomes, or anything we cannot guarantee.
- "Tax-free" without specifying which tax. Panama has property tax, transfer tax, ITBMS, etc. Loose "tax-free" is misleading.
- Emojis (the brand voice does not use them).

## Competitor names that must NEVER appear in our content
- International Living
- Live and Invest Overseas
- Encuentra24
- Vivanuncios
- Any specific competing Panama brokerage by name
(If a reader-side comparison is unavoidable, use "other expat-focused publishers" or "listing portals" without naming.)

## Regulated claims we cannot make
We are not attorneys, accountants, or financial advisors. Every article touching these areas must include an attorney/professional-consult line:

- **Legal / residency / visa**: "consult a licensed Panamanian immigration attorney for your specific case." Never assert that a buyer will qualify, that processing will take X days, or that any program is "guaranteed" to continue at its current terms. Visa programs change.
- **Tax**: "consult a Panamanian tax attorney or CPA for your specific situation." Never give a personal tax projection or a calculation that depends on the buyer's home-country tax treatment.
- **Mortgage / financing**: "rates, LTV, and eligibility are set by the lender and change frequently. Confirm directly with the bank." Never publish a specific rate as if it were guaranteed.
- **Investment return / ROI**: never project a specific yield as "expected" or "guaranteed." Where we cite historical or comparable yields, frame them as historical and attribute the source. "Past performance does not guarantee future results."
- **Property value / appraisal**: never publish a number as the "value" of a specific unit unless we have a citable appraisal or recent comparable transaction.

## Disclosures we MUST include
- **Author byline** on every article (David Aguirre or Jesbelyn Gutierrez or designated author). No anonymous publishing.
- **Last-updated date** on every article (visible to reader, plus in schema).
- **Buyer-agency disclosure** when an article discusses how to choose a broker / agent: "panamarealestateguide.com operates as a buyer's agency. We represent only the buyer in any transaction we participate in."
- **Affiliate / commercial relationship**: when an article specifically discusses one of the 13 featured projects we have a developer-direct relationship with, that relationship must be disclosed in the article. "We have a direct relationship with the developer of [project] and would earn a commission if you reserve through us."

## Formatting rules
- Currency: always "USD $XXX" on first mention (multilingual audience, US$ vs CO$ vs MX$ disambiguation)
- Numbers: use commas for thousands in US English ("USD $480,000"); use periods in ES/PT ("USD $480.000")
- Dates in body prose: "April 22, 2026" (English), "22 de abril de 2026" (Spanish)
- Dates in tables / schema / sitemaps: ISO format (2026-04-22)
- Headings: sentence case, not Title Case ("How to buy property in Panama" not "How To Buy Property In Panama")
- No all-caps headings
- Place names: keep Spanish accents in all languages (Pedasí, Coclé, Boquete, Casco Viejo)
