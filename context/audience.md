# Audience

## Primary segments (in order of revenue priority)
1. US retirees (50+) using Pensionado visa for tax-favorable retirement, low cost of living
2. LATAM HNW investors (CO, MX, BR, ES) seeking USD-denominated property as a hedge against local currency volatility and political risk
3. Remote-work expats (30-45) attracted by Friendly Nations visa + lifestyle

## Typical ticket size
- USD $300k and up
- Sweet spot for US retirees: turnkey condo in a managed building under $600k
- LATAM HNW: $500k to $1.5M for prime-zone units (Costa del Este, Punta Pacifica, Santa María)

## What they already know
- Panama is dollarized (no FX risk for USD earners)
- Property prices are below Miami / Costa Rica for comparable quality
- Friendly Nations + Pensionado are the two main residency paths
- Cost of living is a key reason to consider Panama (especially for US retirees)

## What brings them to us
- Specific question they can't find a clean answer to anywhere else (e.g. "what's the real property tax for a $500k condo in Costa del Este?", "is Pedasí really worth the drive vs. Coronado?", "can I get a mortgage as a Colombian buyer?")
- Project comparison they can't get from a Panama broker (most brokers only sell one or two projects)

## What they've tried that didn't work
- Vivanuncios / Encuentra24 (listing portals, no curation, mostly resale)
- Generic expat sites (International Living, Live & Invest Overseas), good for "should I move?" but useless for "which Panama project should I reserve?"
- WhatsApp brokers who push one project hard regardless of fit

## What they hate
- Hard-sell broker tactics ("price going up next week!")
- Vague pricing ("from $XXXk" with no actual unit availability)
- Stock-photo property pages with no floor plan or real photos
- English content that's obviously translated from Spanish badly
- Buyer guides that don't disclose author conflicts (broker-owned content)
