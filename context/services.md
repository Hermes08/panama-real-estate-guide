# Services

## What we sell
1. **Buyer representation** for international and local buyers acquiring property in Panama. We act as a buyer's agency (single agency, not dual). We negotiate, vet the developer / seller, manage paperwork to closing.
2. **Developer-direct reservations** on the 13 featured projects. USD $5,000 refundable deposit reserves a unit while the buyer due-diligences.
3. **Editorial / guide content** as the top-of-funnel: long-form market reports, zone guides, legal/visa explainers (free, not gated).
4. **Cross-segment coverage**: condos, coastal homes, agricultural fincas, operating businesses, commercial. Not single-niche.

## Pricing / commercial model
- Buyer representation: commission paid by the seller (standard Panama market practice). No buyer-side fees on the transaction itself.
- Reservation: USD $5,000 refundable deposit, typical refund window of 15 days (varies by developer). Convertible into the full purchase if buyer proceeds; refunded in full if buyer withdraws within the agreed window.
- Editorial content: free, public, no email gate, no paywall.

## 5 questions we answer over and over (use as FAQ seeds)
1. "Can a foreigner own property in Panama outright?" Yes, no special permit. Title is the same as for a Panamanian.
2. "What's the real total cost of buying (closing costs, taxes, fees)?" Closing costs run approximately 1% of the transaction value (notary, public registry, transfer-related fees), plus annual property tax that depends on price band and exonerations. Always confirm the exact figure with a licensed Panamanian attorney for your specific transaction.
3. "How long does residency take through Friendly Nations or Pensionado?" Friendly Nations: typically 6-12 months from filing. Pensionado: 4-8 months. Both require a licensed Panamanian immigration attorney.
4. "Can I finance as a foreigner?" Yes, some Panamanian banks lend to foreigners, but expect lower LTV (50-60%), higher rates than US/EU, and more documentation. Many buyers pay cash.
5. "What ongoing costs should I budget?" HOA / building fees (varies by project), property tax (with exonerations on new construction for ~20 years in many cases), property management if renting out, and utility/internet at Panama-market rates.
