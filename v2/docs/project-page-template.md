# Project page content template

The structure every project page fills, so all 31 can be written to the same
spec and none of them ship thin.

## Who we are actually competing with

Searching a project name by itself returns **broker listing pages**, not the
developer. For "Pino Alto Boquete": Casa Solution, World Panama Real Estate,
Metro Realty, two RE/MAX franchises, Fischer & Cie, DoPanama, Alex Barrelier.
For "Bioma Costa del Este": Panama Equity, Metro Realty, RE/MAX Millenium,
Business Panama, PanaCrypto, The Agency, The Panama Link, Property Portal.

That is good news and bad news. Those pages are beatable — most are a photo
gallery, a paragraph of developer marketing, and a contact form. But there are
eight to twelve of them per project, they are established, and several are on
domains with far more authority than ours.

**We do not win by being another listing.** We win on two things they
structurally cannot do: a full per-unit price and size table, and an honest
assessment including what is wrong with the project.

## What they carry that we currently do not

Observed on the ranking pages, absent from our data:

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Developer | Empresas Bern (Pino Alto) · The Velopers (Bioma) |
| Architect | Mallol Arquitectos (Bioma) |
| Building structure | 3 phases × 34 units · 60-storey tower |
| Unit range in both units | 56–131 m² (603–1,410 sq ft) |
| Delivery by phase | 2026 → late 2027 |
| Location context | "Avenida Las Costas, opposite Town Center mall" |
| Financing | "Owner financing available" |
| **The hook** | **Pino Alto is the only development in Panama with full Tourism Authority approval for short-term rentals** |

That last row matters most. The tourism licence *is* the reason anyone searches
Pino Alto — it is what makes legal Airbnb possible — and our page does not
mention it. Every project has one fact like this. Finding it is the writing job.

⚠️ Our Airtable figures for Pino Alto ($245k–$395k, 2 unit types, from 100 m²)
disagree with the ranking pages ($200k–$400k, 56–131 m²). Either we hold one
phase and they hold another, or ours is stale. **Reconcile before publishing** —
a price that contradicts eight other sites reads as carelessness.

## The template

Sections in this order. Each maps to a question the searcher already has, and
the H2 carries the project name so the page stays on-entity throughout.

### 1. Identity (above the fold)
Name · developer · architect · area · status · delivery · price range · unit
count. Mostly rendered from structured fields, not prose.

### 2. The hook — one paragraph
The single specific thing that makes this project different from the tower next
door. A tourism licence, a titled-vs-ROP position, beachfront that is actually
beachfront, a school within walking distance. If you cannot name one, say the
project is unremarkable and explain what it competes on instead. **This is the
paragraph that stops the page reading like a syndicated listing.**

### 3. `{Name} prices and floor plans`
The per-unit table — name, beds, baths, m², price. Already built, and the one
thing no competitor publishes. Add sq ft alongside m² for US buyers.

### 4. `Where {Name} actually is`
Not coordinates — context. What is walkable, how far to Tocumen, which
supermarket, what the road in is like, what gets noisy. Links to the area page.

### 5. `What's included at {Name}`
Full amenity list, in English. Ours are still in Spanish from Airtable.

### 6. `Who {Name} suits — and who it doesn't`
The section none of the brokers will ever write, because they earn on the sale.
Retirees vs investors vs families; the climate; the commute; the resale risk of
a 60-storey tower in a market with heavy new supply. **Naming a real drawback
is what makes the rest credible.**

### 7. `Before you buy at {Name}`
Title status, permits, the developer's delivery record, deposit structure,
what an attorney should pull. Already built as a component.

### 8. FAQ
Five to eight real questions, marked up as `FAQPage`. Pull them from what
people actually ask: is it titled, can I rent it short-term, what are the
closing costs, can foreigners buy, does it qualify for residency.

### 9. Lead form + related projects
Already built.

## Minimum bar before publishing a project page

- The hook paragraph exists and is specific to this project
- Prices reconciled against at least two external sources
- Amenities translated
- At least five FAQs
- One named drawback in section 6
- 700+ words of original prose, none of it from `Descripción EN`

Below that bar the page is a listing, and there are already ten of those per
project on domains older than ours.
